PETROGRAPHY / 02

THE
MICRO SCALE.

A geological material can reveal far more than its hand specimen appearance. Petrography examines the relationships inside it.

01

COMPOSITION

Mineral constituents and other microscopic components, identified in the context of the material and purpose.

02

TEXTURE & FABRIC

Grain size, shape, boundaries, orientation, matrix or cement, and relationships between constituents.

03

ALTERATION & WEATHERING

Microscopic evidence of alteration, weathering, coatings, reaction features and related changes.

04

MICROSTRUCTURES

Fractures, microcracks, inclusions, veins, voids and other features relevant to the investigation.
