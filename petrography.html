PETROGRAPHY / 01

THE
MICRO
SCALE.

Petrography reads geological materials through their microscopic composition, texture, fabric and internal relationships.

EXAMINATION

WHAT THE
SECTION REVEALS.

A

MINERAL CONSTITUENTS

Identification and description of mineral constituents and relevant non-mineral components.

B

TEXTURE / FABRIC

Grain relationships, boundaries, size and shape, matrix or cement, orientation and overall fabric.

C

ALTERATION

Weathering, alteration products, coatings, reaction rims and related microscopic evidence where present.

D

FEATURES

Fractures, microcracks, inclusions, voids, veins and other project-relevant microfeatures.

SCOPE

SAMPLE +
PURPOSE.

The sample and project purpose define the examination. Applicable standards or project specifications can be incorporated where relevant.
